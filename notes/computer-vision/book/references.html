References

Slide numbers in the text count from the title slide of each deck.

Course materials (CS 585, Spring 2024)

  • M. Betke, CS 585 Image and Video Computing course page and schedule.
  • D. Theriault and M. Betke, Program with Images: Pitfalls (Lecture notes 1).
  • M. Betke, Face detection, image differencing, template matching, SSD, NCC and pyramids, January 30, 2024 (Lecture notes 2).
  • The KidsRoom: A Perceptually-Based Interactive and Immersive Story Environment, slides on the work of A. Bobick et al. (Lecture notes 3).
  • M. Betke, Projections, multi-object labeling and face detection, January 25, 2024.
  • M. Betke, Segmentation, boundary following, Hausdorff distance and curvature, January 30 and February 1, 2024 (Lecture notes 4, used here only for the thresholding methods and the greyscale formula).

Books and papers

  • B. K. P. Horn, Robot Vision, MIT Press, 1986. Chapter 3 for area, centroid, orientation and the second moments. Chapter 4 for connectivity and sequential labelling.
  • R. Szeliski, Computer Vision: Algorithms and Applications, 2nd edition, Springer, 2022. Sections 2.3.1 and 2.3.2 for sampling and colour, Section 3.5 for pyramids, Section 9.1 for translational alignment and hierarchical search.
  • W. T. Freeman et al., Computer Vision for Interactive Computer Graphics, IEEE Computer Graphics and Applications, May–June 1998, pp. 42–53. Sidebar 2.1 for moments and projections, Section 2 for motion energy, Section 5 and Sidebar 5.1 for running-average backgrounds and normalized correlation.
  • A. F. Bobick, S. S. Intille, J. W. Davis, F. Baird, C. S. Pinhanez, L. W. Campbell, Y. A. Ivanov, A. Schütte and A. Wilson, The KidsRoom: A Perceptually-Based Interactive and Immersive Story Environment, Presence: Teleoperators and Virtual Environments 8(4), 369–393, 1999.
  • A. F. Bobick and J. W. Davis, The Recognition of Human Movement Using Temporal Templates, IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence 23(3), 257–267, 2001. Section 3 for motion-energy and motion-history images.
  • T. W. Ridler and S. Calvard, Picture thresholding using an iterative selection method, IEEE Transactions on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics 8(8), 630–632, 1978.
  • L. Shapiro and G. Stockman, Computer Vision, Prentice Hall, 2001. Source of the normalized red and green skin-colour plot, as credited on slide 16 of the January 25 deck.
